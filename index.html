Private CSV field visit map planner for sales teams

Pratix Field Visit Map turns a CSV list of customers, prospects, stores or service locations into a visual route-planning workspace. It is built for sales representatives, technicians, merchandisers and local operators who need a quick map without sending business data to a server.

Useful field-work scenarios

  • Group customer visits by territory, priority or account type.
  • Give a sales team a visual overview before a busy day in the field.
  • Review technician, installer or inspection locations.
  • Export a GeoJSON file for another mapping or planning workflow.

CSV format

Use columns such as name, lat, lng, category, notes. The file is read locally in the browser, pins are grouped on the map and notes stay available in each marker. A demo dataset is included so teams can understand the workflow before importing their own file.

Built for privacy and speed

There is no account, backend or address database. That makes the planner a practical first step for small businesses that need a visual territory view but do not want to configure a full CRM mapping product.

Frequently asked questions

Does the CSV leave my device?No. It is parsed locally in your browser.
Can I group locations?Yes. The category column is used to summarize and display visit groups.
Can I search for a city?Yes. Use the place search to center the map before loading or reviewing pins.
What is GeoJSON?It is an open geographic format that can be reused in many mapping tools.
